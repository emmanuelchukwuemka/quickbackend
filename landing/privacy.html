Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

QuickDrop ("QuickDrop", "we", "us", or "our") operates the QuickDrop mobile application and website (together, the "Service"), connecting passengers with drivers for local rides in Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and a password (stored in encrypted form). Drivers additionally provide date of birth, gender, residential address, state and LGA, an emergency contact's name and phone number, and a profile photo.

Driver verification documents

To verify a driver's identity and eligibility to operate on QuickDrop, we collect a National Identification Number (NIN), driver's license details and expiry date, vehicle information (make, model, year, colour, plate number, registration number, seat count), and photos of the driver's license, NIN slip, a utility bill, the vehicle's registration document, an insurance document, and photos of the vehicle itself. These documents are reviewed by our team before a driver is approved to accept rides.

Location information

We collect precise location data from your device while you use the Service, to match passengers with nearby drivers, calculate fares and routes, and show live trip progress on the map. Drivers' location is shared with their matched passenger for the duration of a trip. We do not collect location data when the app is not in use for an active ride or driver availability.

Trip and communications data

We keep a record of your rides — pickup and drop-off points, fare, timestamps, and ratings — so you and drivers can view ride history and we can resolve disputes. In-app chat messages between a driver and passenger are relayed to enable communication about the trip. In-app voice calls are connected directly between the driver's and passenger's devices; QuickDrop does not record or store the audio content of these calls.

Payment information

Passengers are not charged through the app. Drivers fund an in-app wallet to pay ride commissions, and wallet top-ups are processed by our payment partner, Paystack. QuickDrop does not receive or store your card, bank, or other payment credentials — these are handled entirely by Paystack under its own privacy policy. We retain a record of wallet transactions (amount, date, status) for accounting purposes.

Device and usage information

We collect a device push-notification token (used to deliver ride, chat, and call alerts) and basic usage information such as app version and general diagnostic logs, to keep the Service reliable and secure.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify drivers before they can accept rides
  • To match passengers with nearby available drivers and calculate fares
  • To show live trip tracking and enable in-app chat and calls between a matched driver and passenger
  • To process driver wallet top-ups and commission deductions
  • To send ride-related notifications (offers, driver arrival, trip updates) and account notices
  • To investigate complaints, disputes, and suspected misuse of the Service
  • To improve the reliability, safety, and performance of the Service

How We Share Information

We share the minimum information necessary for a ride to happen: a passenger's name, pickup location, and phone number are shared with the driver assigned to their ride, and a driver's name, photo, vehicle details, rating, and location are shared with the passenger they're matched with. We also share information with:

  • Paystack, to process driver wallet top-ups
  • Firebase (Google), to deliver push notifications and for core app infrastructure
  • Google Maps, to provide mapping, routing, and location-matching features
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law or to protect the safety of our users

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain account and trip information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. Driver verification documents are retained for as long as required to demonstrate regulatory compliance.

Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update most of your account information from within the app. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below. Note that we may need to retain certain records (such as trip history tied to unresolved disputes or legal obligations) even after a deletion request.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect information from, anyone under the age of 18. Both passenger and driver accounts require the account holder to be an adult.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including encrypted network connections and password hashing — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the app.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at support@quickdrop.ng.
